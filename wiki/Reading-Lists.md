# Reading Lists

A **Reading List** is a hand-ordered sequence of issues: a reading order, a story arc, a crossover event. Unlike
[Smart Lists](Smart-Lists), you choose the contents and the order.

Open **Reading Lists** from the navigation rail.

## The gallery

The section opens on a gallery of your lists.

- **Continue reading** at the top shows the lists you've started and can keep reading, most recent first. Click one to jump
  straight to where you are.
- Below it, every **folder** and **list** is a tile: a list shows its cover (or a mosaic of its first covers) and how much you've
  read; a folder shows a mosaic of its lists and its overall progress.
- Click a folder to go inside it; the breadcrumb at the top takes you back out.
- **Tag chips** (#crisis, #x-men…) filter every list with that tag, whatever folder it is in.
- **＋ Folder** and **＋ New list** create things in the folder you're looking at. **⋯** imports a whole folder of `.cbl` files
  (sub-folders become folders) or exports this folder the same way.
- **Drag** a tile to reorder it, or drop it onto a folder to move it inside. **Right-click** a tile to rename, move, export or
  delete it. Deleting a folder never deletes its lists; they move up a level.

## Reading a list

Opening a list shows:

- a **cover rail** on the left with the other lists in the same folder (click to switch, **⌂** for the gallery);
- a **hero** with the list's cover, name, tags, progress and, once you've read a few comics, an estimate of when you'll finish it
  at your recent pace. **▶ Continue** opens the next unread issue;
- the **path**: the issues in reading order along a line that fills in as you read. Sub-arcs are **chapters** (click a chapter to
  fold it away; chapters you've finished start folded). The next issue to read is expanded into an **Up next** card. The list opens
  scrolled to where you are.

Click an issue to read it; the reader's previous/next follows the list's order. A **missing** issue offers **Find & link** (pick the
real comic from your library) or **Request** it.

**Path | Covers** at the top right switches to a wall of covers, grouped by chapter; the app remembers your choice.

## Editing a list

Click **✎ Edit list**:

- drag the **⠿** handle to reorder (or use **Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓**); dragging into another chapter moves the issue into it;
- tick issues to act on several at once: set a role, move them to a chapter, mark read or unread, or remove them;
- **＋ Chapter from selection** turns the ticked issues into a new chapter; click ✎ on a chapter to rename it;
- set each issue's role, add a note, or remove it.

Press **Done** (or Esc) to go back to reading.

**＋ Add issues** opens a panel on the right: search your library and add issues (or a whole series), or switch to **Story arc** to
build from a published arc.

## Checks

A strip under the header appears when there's something to act on:

- **Overlap**: this list shares most of its issues with another one. **Compare** them side by side, **Merge** them, or say they're
  **Not a duplicate**.
- **ComicVine / Metron**: for a list linked to a story event, **Check against ComicVine** (in ⋯) compares it with the published
  order. **Insert missing** adds what it lacks; **Reorder** puts it in the published order. Nothing is ever removed.
- **Rebuilt**: after rebuilding a list made from a continuity, what changed.

## The ⋯ menu

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Import .CBL / .CSV** | import a list file |
| **Build from a story arc** | look an arc up online (ComicVine, Metron, and reading-order sites) and build a list from it |
| **Link story event** | tie the list to one of your [Story Events](Story-Events-and-Relations) |
| **Refresh from source** / **Follow this arc** | for arc lists: re-check the arc now, or automatically every day |
| **Rebuild from continuity** | for lists made from a continuity: rebuild it (your notes and roles stay) |
| **Detect roles** | mark issues as prologue, core, tie-in, epilogue… from their titles and formats |
| **Request missing issues** | for arc lists: ask for every issue you don't have |
| **Export** | `.cbl`, `.csv`, plain text, a **printable checklist (PDF)**, or copy as text |
| **Edit details** | name, type, description, cover and tags |

You can also make a list from a continuity: on the continuity's page, **⋯ → Create reading list** in **story order** (as the
continuity map reads) or **publication order**.
