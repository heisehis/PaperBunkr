# Caption templates

The words that go out *with* each card, one template per series, paired with the card layout in `README.md`.

**The rule for every series: the card carries the statement, the caption carries what the card can't.** A caption never
restates the card. It adds the hook, the context or story, and one ask. (From the Social Media Skills `caption-writer`:
the first line decides whether the rest gets read; one goal, one call to action; complement the visual, don't describe it.)

How to use: copy the series' block into a new draft in `social/drafts/`, fill the `[slots]`, write one alternative X
first line under `## X alternatives`, and check the X length (280; a link counts as 23, an emoji as 2).

---

## 1. Drop Day · card `drop` · goal: clicks to the official read

| Card carries | Caption carries |
| --- | --- |
| OUT NOW, series + chapter, official art | Why this chapter matters (spoiler-free), where to read it |

First-line patterns: "[Series] [N] is OUT 🔥 and [spoiler-free stake]." · "[Character] has been building to this for [N] chapters." ·
"If you stopped reading [Series] at [arc], this is the week to come back."

```x
[Series] Chapter [N] is OUT 🔥 [one spoiler-free line on what's at stake].

Read it free on [official source]: [link]
```
```telegram
[Series] Chapter [N] is out 🔥

[One or two spoiler-free lines on what's at stake.]

Read it free on [official source]: [link]
```
Spoilers: X → a reply starting "spoilers below 👇"; Telegram → a second message starting "⚠️ Spoilers".

## 2. Explained 🧵 · card `take` (headline as title) · goal: reads + reposts

| Card carries | Caption carries |
| --- | --- |
| The news in one line | The thread: what changed, what it means, what's still unknown |

```x
[News in one line] and the timeline is confused. Here's what's actually changing, not the outrage version 🧵
```
Posts 2-5: one fact each, sources named. Last post: "What it means for readers: [one line]." Telegram: the whole thread
as one post with `•` bullets and the source links inline.

## 3. Hot Take Tuesday · card `take` · goal: replies

| Card carries | Caption carries |
| --- | --- |
| The take, word for word, + "Agree or disagree?" | The evidence or the confession behind it, then a specific ask |

First-line patterns: "Every [genre]: [the formula in one sentence]." · "I've read [N] of these and [admission]." ·
"[Popular thing] isn't [what people say]. It's [your take]."

```x
[The pattern or evidence behind the take, concrete, one or two lines.]

[Your honest admission or twist.]

[Specific ask: "Which one hooked you hardest?" / "Name the exception."] 👇
```
```telegram
Hot Take Tuesday 🔥 Vote below, then [specific ask].
```
```telegram-poll
[The take as a question, under 300 characters]
- Agree, and I love it
- Agree, and I'm tired of it
- Disagree, [the strongest counter]
- [Never read / watched one]
```
