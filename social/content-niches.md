# Daily posting niches: @Ehis_II + Telegram

Ten recurring series for posting every day on the main X account, each with a Telegram version: fan series (1-8) go to
the **@nerd9ja_hub** channel, Paperbunkr series (9-10) to the **Updates topic** of the @Paperbunkr group. Built with the Social Media Skills method: a few
**themes** (pillars), each delivered through repeatable **series** so there's never a blank "what do I post?" day.

## The four pillars

| Pillar | Share of posts | Why you own it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Comics** (Western) | ~25% | You read and organise them daily; you built a library app around them |
| **Manga** | ~25% | Same, plus right-to-left reading is something you've had to get right in code |
| **Manhwa / webtoons** | ~25% | Vertical-scroll reading, the fastest-growing of the three |
| **Paperbunkr** | ~25% | Your product; build-in-public and tips. Rides on the trust the other three build |

**Signature angle:** *one reader of all three formats*. Few accounts talk about comics, manga and manhwa side by side; you
build software that has to handle all three. Series 7 (Format Wars) is where that shows most.

## Series details and sample posts

Ready-to-post samples are marked **Ready**. **Template** ones need today's facts filled in, and those facts have to be
checked before posting (chapter numbers, dates, prices).

### 1. Drop Day
A chapter or issue just came out. Official image, spoiler-free hook, link to the **legal** place to read it.

Template:
```text
[Series] Chapter [N] is OUT 🔥

[One spoiler-free line about the chapter.]

Read it free on [official source]: [link]
```
- Spoilers go in a reply under "spoilers below 👇", never in the main post.
- Only link official sources (Shonen Jump, Manga Plus, Webtoon, Tapas, publisher sites). No scanlation or pirate links.

### 2. Explained 🧵
Big news, sorted out. Opener + 3-5 posts + "what it means for readers".

Template (opener):
```text
[News in one line] and the timeline is confused. Here's what's actually changing, not the outrage version 🧵
```

### 3. Hot Take Tuesday
One opinion, a reason, and an invitation to disagree. Argue things you actually believe; punch at tropes, never at creators.

Ready:
```text
Hot take: manhwa system windows are just shonen training arcs with a UI.

Same grind, same power-ups, same rival who's always one level ahead. And I'm still hooked every time.

Agree or disagree?
```
Ready:
```text
Hot take: the filler arc you remember fondly is worth more than the canon arc you skimmed.

Name yours. 👇
```

### 4. This or That
A forced choice. Reply-based (Buffer can't post X polls; to use a real poll, post it from the X app).

Ready:
```text
Pick one, forever:

🅰️ Weekly chapters, 15 to 20 pages
🅱️ Monthly chapters, 40+ pages

Reply A or B.
```
Ready:
```text
You can only read one for the rest of the year:

📕 Comics
📗 Manga
📘 Manhwa

Choose. No "all three" answers. 😤
```

### 5. Rate the Cover
An official cover or key visual, credited, and a 1-10 ask.

Template:
```text
Rate this cover 1 to 10.

[Series] #[N], art by [artist] ([publisher]).
```
- Official promo art only, always credited. Never scan interior pages.

### 6. Starter Pack
Where to start, three picks, one line each, then ask for additions.

Ready:
```text
New to manhwa? Start with one of these:

• Solo Leveling: the weakest hunter gets a mysterious system
• Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint: the only reader of a web novel that comes true
• Tower of God: a boy follows a girl up a tower where every floor is a test

What would you add?
```
More packs to rotate: manga for people who only watch anime, comics for manga readers, horror across all three,
completed series you can binge in a weekend.

### 7. Format Wars (signature)
Comics vs manga vs manhwa: reading direction, colour, release rhythm, page vs scroll. Questions, not lectures.

Ready:
```text
Comics read left to right.
Manga reads right to left.
Manhwa scrolls top to bottom.

Which one did you learn first, and which one still trips you up?
```
More angles: colour vs black-and-white, monthly issues vs weekly chapters vs daily episodes, collected volumes vs
reading online, "panels" vs "scroll pacing".

### 8. Reader's Desk
Your week in reading, ideally with a screenshot of your Paperbunkr library (soft product exposure, no pitch).

Template:
```text
What I read this week:

📕 [comic]
📗 [manga]
📘 [manhwa]

Best panel goes to [one], no contest. What did you read?
```

### 9. Build Log (Paperbunkr)
One thing you made or fixed, why a reader cares, and a screenshot/GIF if there is one. Also goes to the Telegram Updates topic.

Ready:
```text
Small Paperbunkr fix this week: if syncing your reading progress fails (wifi blinks, the tracker is down), it now waits and retries instead of dropping it.

Losing your place 400 chapters into a manga hurts. That shouldn't happen anymore.
```

### 10. Paperbunkr Tip
One feature, how to use it, link last. Also goes to the Telegram Updates topic.

Ready:
```text
Paperbunkr tip: press G while reading and guided view walks the page panel by panel, so a full comic page reads comfortably on a small laptop screen.

Free for Windows: https://github.com/heisehis/PaperBunkr
```

## Tailoring for Telegram

Telegram isn't X with more room. What changes:

| | X | Telegram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 280 per post, threads for more | One post up to 4096 (1024 with media): a thread becomes **one long post** |
| Links | Last post / reply (links in the opener cut reach) | Anywhere; put the read/download link right in the post |
| Engagement | Replies | **Native polls** (replies only work where comments are on). Every engagement series uses a poll |
| Spoilers | Reply under "spoilers below 👇" | Separate follow-up message starting "⚠️ Spoilers", sent a minute later |
| Hashtags | At most one | None |
| Formatting | Plain | Plain text and `•` bullets (the script sends no formatting) |

In a draft file, a poll goes under a `## Telegram poll` heading (question line, then `- option` lines);
`social/post-telegram.ps1` sends the post, then the poll. Fan posts need `-Channel @nerd9ja_hub`.

Telegram samples for the series where the shape changes:

**Build Log** (Updates topic): the X text plus one or two sentences on how it works, and the download link

**Paperbunkr Tip** (Updates topic): the X text turned into steps:
```text

• Open a comic in single-page mode
• Press G to turn guided view on
```

(Checked against the app: `G` is the default key, guided view needs paged single-page layout.)

## Voice for fan posts (series 1-8)

Taken from what already works on your account:
- News first, then the hook. CAPS for one or two words of emphasis ("OUT", "RIGHT NOW"), not whole sentences.
- 🔥 for drops, 🧵 for threads, 👇 for "replies below". Up to three emojis.
- End with a question. Engagement posts live or die on the reply.
- Official images with alt text, credited.

## Engagement rules

Engagement bait is fine when it starts a real conversation. Never:
- fake giveaways, "RT to win" without a real prize, or follow-for-follow;
- ragebait aimed at creators, translators or other fans;
- spoilers in the main post of anything less than a week old;
- pirate or scanlation links, or uploaded interior pages.
