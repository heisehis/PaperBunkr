# Home Dashboard & Recommendations

**Home** (navigation rail → *Home*) is the landing screen. It pulls together where you
are and what to read next.

## Modules

### Continue Reading

Issues you have in progress, most recent first, each with a **Read Now** button that drops
you back exactly where you stopped. Empty until you've started something.

### Recently Added

The newest issues to enter your library, with **View Library →** to see the rest.

### Because you read *X*

Recommendations seeded from a series you've read, using PaperBunkr's relationship-aware
recommendation engine. **This needs data to work:** it draws on the **related series**,
**continuities**, and **story events** you've linked from series
[detail pages](Story-Events-and-Relations). With nothing linked yet, it shows a prompt
instead of picks. **Refresh** re-runs it.

### Try This Reading List

Spotlights a [Reading List](Reading-Lists) that still has unread issues, with **Start the
list →**.

### Spotlight carousel

A rotating highlight from your library (auto-advances every few seconds).

### Search

The Home search box jumps straight to a series, or you scroll down to discover from the
modules.

## Getting better recommendations

1. Open a series you like → its **detail page**.
2. Use the **Related** tab → **+ Add Related Series** to connect sequels, spin-offs, and
   similar titles.
3. Add series to a **Continuity** or a **Story Event** where it fits.
4. Come back to Home and **Refresh** the *Because you read* module.
