## Telegram

Stop scrolling your library to find one series.

Press Ctrl+P on any screen and type what you're after:

• a series or an issue
• a book
• a reading list or smart list
• a screen like Library or Wanted

Arrow to it, press Enter, and you're there.

Download: https://github.com/heisehis/PaperBunkr/releases/latest
