# Books (EPUB & PDF)

**Books** (navigation rail → *Books*) is a separate section for **EPUB and novel-style
PDFs**, kept apart from the comic/manga Library because they read differently (reflowable
text, not fixed pages).

## Adding books

Books have their **own folder list**, independent of the comic library:

1. Open **Books**.
2. In the **BOOK FOLDERS** panel, click **Add Folder** and pick a folder of `.epub` /
   `.pdf` files.
3. Click **Scan Now**.

Imported books show as cards with title and author, grouped by series where the metadata
provides one. Right-click a book for **Delete Book** (with a confirm step).

## The book reader

Open a book to get a text reader tuned for prose:

- **Paging** — on-screen `‹` / `›`, arrow keys, or click the page edges. A **progress %**
  is shown and your position is saved.
- **☰ Table of contents / Chapters** — jump to any chapter.
- **🔍 Search this book** — full-text search with excerpts; jump to any match.
- **🔖 Bookmarks** — bookmark the current page; the Bookmarks panel lists them all.
- **Aa — Font and theme:**
  - **TEXT SIZE** — `A` / `A` smaller/larger
  - **FONT** — Sans / Serif / Mono
  - **LINE SPACING** — Compact / Normal / Relaxed
  - **THEME** — light/sepia/dark presets, or **Match app skin**

## Novel PDFs vs comic PDFs

A PDF can be read either way. Page-image PDFs (scanned comics) belong in the **Library**
and open in the page reader; text PDFs (novels) belong in **Books** and open in the text
reader. If a PDF lands in the wrong place, move the file to the other section's folder and
re-scan.
