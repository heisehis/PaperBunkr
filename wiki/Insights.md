# Insights

**Insights** (navigation rail) shows what and how you read: what you're in the middle of, goals you've
set, and how your reading and your library change over time. It has three tabs.

## Today

- **Goals** at the top, each with a progress ring and whether you're on pace. **Add goal** opens
  *New reading goal*: choose what to **track** (for example issues or pages), a **target**, a
  **period** (such as this year or this month) and a **scope** (the whole library, one series, one
  publisher or one genre). You get a notice when a goal passes 50% and when it's met.
- **Continuing** and **Reading**: what you're partway through, with covers, one click from the reader.
- **Almost done**: series a couple of issues from finished. **Dive in**: full runs you own and haven't
  started.
- **Recommendations** based on series you recently *finished* (Home's *Because you read* uses what you
  recently opened), with a line saying why each one was picked.
- **Collection health**: gaps in runs you own.

## Trends

Charts over a range you choose, with **Export**:

- **Finished · range**, **Pace** and **Avg to finish**, each with a change badge against the previous
  period of the same length (hidden on *All time*, or when there isn't enough history yet).
- **Backlog burn-down**: your unread count over time, from a snapshot taken once a day, and where it's
  heading. It needs about a week of snapshots before it draws a trend.
- **Activity heatmap**, **Day streak** and **Finish streak**, **Reading pace**.
- **Library growth**, **Media type**, **Reading state**, **Content rating**, **Publication year**,
  **Score distribution**, and top **publishers**, **genres**, **tags**, **authors** and **artists**.
- **Highlights**: longest journey, fastest completion, most reread, highest rated.

## Recap

A year-in-review in slides: items finished, pages read, your longest streak, busiest day, top series,
writer and artist, highest-rated series and most-reread item. The **Recap** tab appears only at the
end of the year (29–31 December), like a yearly "wrapped".
