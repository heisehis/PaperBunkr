PaperBunkr

A free, local-first desktop comic & manga library and reader for Windows — a ground-up rewrite of ComicRack. Your library stays on your machine: no cloud, no account.

Beta — solo project, expect rough edges

What it does

A full library manager and a custom reader — not a viewer, not a web service.

Comic & manga reading

Real page rendering for CBZ / CBR / PDF. Fit modes, zoom, rotation, page transitions, double-page spreads, continuous / webtoon scroll, auto-scroll, and right-to-left manga direction.

Books

A separate section for novels — EPUB, PDF, FB2, and MOBI/AZW3 — with a reflow reader, a shared reading HUD, in-text highlights and notes, and accessibility support.

Library management

Series and per-issue browsing, virtualized view modes, Collections (manual or rule-based), unified sort/group/filter, Virtual Tags, Saved Workspaces, and full keyboard navigation.

Home dashboard

Continue reading, "because you read…" recommendations, and spotlight modules, driven by a relationship-aware recommendation engine.

Metadata & editing

Single and bulk issue editors, series relations, continuity groupings, story events, and online lookups from AniList, MangaBaka, MangaUpdates, MangaDex, Kitsu, and ComicVine.

Smart Lists & Reading Lists

Saved rule-based views with nested AND/OR groups, plus hand-curated and imported (CBL / CSV) reading lists with story-arc lookup.

Insights

A reading-habit dashboard and a library/reading analytics view, backed by an append-only log of your reading activity.

Library Health & Automation

Find missing files and likely duplicates, and run background maintenance — rescans, cover verification, backups — on a schedule you control.

Plugins

An installable plugin ecosystem with its own package manager, a plugin API, Python plugin commands, and a built-in Duplicate Finder.

A look around

Library, a series detail screen, and reading insights.

Formats

Comics and books, read natively — no conversion step.

Get started

Three steps from download to reading.

1

Grab the installer from the latest release and run it. It bundles its own .NET runtime — nothing else to install.

2

Point PaperBunkr at your comics folder. It scans the files, folds trade paperbacks, and builds a local library database alongside them.

3

Coming from ComicRack CE? Import your existing library — database, reading state, and lists — from the migration screen.

Full docs are in the project wiki; build-from-source instructions are in the README.
