# Comparing two copies

When you have two copies of the same comic (a duplicate group, or two scans of one issue) you can put
them side by side and pick the better one.

**Open it:** select exactly **two** comics in the Library, right-click and choose **Compare files…**,
or press **Compare** on a duplicate group in Library Health. Both must be files on this computer.

**What you see**

- **Side by side** (default) or **flicker**: press `M` to switch. In flicker mode `Space` (or `F`)
  flips between A and B in exactly the same place, so a difference jumps out.
- Pages are paired **by picture**, not by number, so a copy with an extra cover or an ad still lines
  up. The header says whether the page was matched. If the automatic pairing keeps missing, the **−**
  and **+** buttons shift B's pairing by a page (remembered for the visit).
- Zooming and panning move both sides together. `0` resets the view.
- Under each copy: page size, file size, format, page count and bytes per page, with badges (*Higher
  resolution*, *Larger file*, *More pages*) on the copy that wins each.

**Deciding**

- **Keep A** (`1`) or **Keep B** (`2`) asks first, naming both files, then moves the other to the
  Recycle Bin.
- **Not a duplicate** keeps both and stops listing them as duplicates.
- **Back** (`Esc`) leaves without changing anything.

Keys: `Left`/`Right` or `PageUp`/`PageDown` turn the page, `Home` goes to the first page.
